Privacy policy for iOS

Updated: 18 October 2026 · Version 0.3.18

This policy explains how the iOS game Sottosopra, developed by Ihar Salyanchuk (EdwardTeach), handles information.

Local play and cloud services

The game stores progress, settings and the current run on your device. Save files you export remain wherever you choose to store them. When cloud services are enabled, Firebase Authentication creates a game account, initially anonymous. You may link it to Google or Sign in with Apple to recover that cloud profile on another device. Linking a Google or Apple identity is optional.

Information used for the game

Cloud services process the game account identifier, linked sign-in provider identifiers and the name or email made available by the selected provider. They store cloud saves, progress, achievements, run results and your chosen nickname. This information is used to sign you in, synchronize progress, operate leaderboards and provide gameplay features.

A leaderboard nickname and result, and gameplay replays you publish as ghosts, are visible to other players. Do not put personal information in your nickname.

App Store purchases

Apple handles payments. The game and its verification service process product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, ownership and refund status, and signed store proofs to grant and restore purchased content. The developer does not receive payment card details. Purchase proofs are verified against Apple; private developer keys are not included in the game.

Service providers, diagnostics and device access

Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, database and server hosting. These services process network and service metadata, including IP addresses and request or error logs, to deliver and protect the service. Sign-in SDKs may process contact details, identifiers, technical diagnostics and usage information for authentication, fraud prevention, operation and their own service analytics. The game uses provider identifiers, name and email for account features; it does not provide phone-number authentication. Google may estimate a general location from an IP address for fraud prevention.

This version contains no advertising SDK or FirebaseAnalytics product. We do not sell player data, use it for targeted advertising, or request camera, microphone, contacts or device location access. Gameplay data is used to operate the game. Apple and Google also handle information under their own policies when you use their services.

Account deletion

Profile → Delete account starts deletion of the Firebase game identity, cloud saves, nickname, leaderboard records and published ghosts. A linked account may require you to sign in again. For Apple-linked accounts, the game also requests revocation of the Apple authorization. Cloud cleanup can continue after the game closes, with automatic retries if a step fails. The initiating device checks the outcome when it reconnects.

Progress already on the phone remains available for local play. After deletion, the game does not create another cloud account until you explicitly reconnect. Uninstalling the game alone does not delete an existing cloud account. Exported save copies must be removed separately.

Retention

Cloud profile and gameplay data are retained while the cloud account exists, until deletion is requested. We retain a limited purchase ledger without the deleted Firebase user identifier to handle refunds, prevent duplicate grants and restore purchases to a replacement game account after fresh store ownership verification. Apple retains its own purchase history. Deleting a game account does not request a refund or cancel an App Store purchase.

A temporary hashed deletion barrier prevents late requests from recreating cloud data and becomes eligible for automatic removal after 24 hours. An anonymous completion receipt containing only a random request identifier and completion status lets a phone returning after a long offline period confirm deletion.

The project's default infrastructure log bucket retains logs for 30 days; its required Google Cloud audit-log bucket retains logs for 400 days. Provider authentication records, backups and other provider-controlled records follow the providers' retention and deletion processes and may not disappear immediately.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact support, the email address, message and attachments you choose to send are used to answer the request and handle applicable obligations. Correspondence is kept only as needed for that purpose. You may request access, correction or deletion of your information using the contact below. Information needed to verify the request may be requested. Contact support if you cannot access the in-game deletion option.

This website and changes

This static policy website uses no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and processes website access logs under its privacy statement. If the game's information handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new date.
