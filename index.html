Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-06

This policy explains how the Android game Sottosopra, developed by EdwardTeach, handles information. The game is intended for people aged 13 and older.

Game data stays on your device

Progress, the current run, settings and unlocked content are stored locally. The game does not create a separate player account or provide a developer-operated cloud save. Save files that you export yourself remain wherever you choose to store them.

Google Play purchases

Where optional purchases are available, Google Play processes payment. The game receives product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, time and signed purchase information to confirm and restore access to purchased content. Payment card details are not provided to the game. Google manages store accounts, payment processing and purchase history under its own policies.

Advertising and device access

The current game has no advertising or analytics SDK. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. We do not sell player data or use it for targeted advertising.

Contacting support

If you email support, your address, message and any attachments you choose to send are used to answer your request. Correspondence is kept only as needed to deal with the request and applicable obligations. You can request deletion using the contact below.

Retention and deletion

You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Copies you exported must be deleted separately. This does not cancel purchases or delete Google's purchase history. Purchased content can be restored through the relevant Google Play account. Questions about information handled by Google should be directed to Google.

This policy website

This static page uses no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement.

Changes and questions

If the game's information handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new date. Please use the contact below for privacy or support questions.
